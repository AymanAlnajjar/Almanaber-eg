# GEO Content Roadmap

Technical discoverability is the foundation; recommendation systems still need clear, verifiable, independently corroborated information.

## Phase 1 — entity consistency

- Approve one official English name, Arabic name, and AMCE abbreviation.
- Reconcile website, Google Business Profile, social profiles, directories, and company documents.
- Publish complete office and contact information.
- Create a concise company facts page with founding details, disciplines, locations, and leadership after verification.

## Phase 2 — service authority

For each core service, publish a substantial bilingual page covering scope, deliverables, project stages, relevant codes, coordination process, common client questions, verifiable example projects, and the responsible expert.

Priority services:

1. Architectural design
2. Structural engineering
3. MEP design
4. Fire and life safety engineering

## Phase 3 — case studies

Create approved bilingual case studies with explicit facts: client or client category, location, building type, AMCE scope, constraints, engineering approach, dates, area, measurable results, and licensed images.

Avoid invented outcomes, client quotes, or awards.

## Phase 4 — expert knowledge

Build a quarterly editorial program around Egyptian and regional engineering questions. Prefer expert-reviewed explanations, checklists, code updates, and project lessons over generic high-volume articles.

## Phase 5 — external corroboration

- Seek accurate profiles in relevant professional and business directories.
- Earn project mentions from clients, partners, industry publications, and event organizers.
- Keep names and URLs consistent.
- Link public author profiles to reviewed technical content.

## Measurement

Track indexed pages, crawl errors, Arabic and English non-brand queries, service-page clicks, referring domains, unlinked brand mentions, exposed AI referrals, and periodic recommendation tests.

Treat AI recommendations as an observed outcome, not a guaranteed ranking target.
